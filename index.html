        About these numbers

        Carb values come from the ISPAD Indian foods carb counting booklet, the "Quick Guide to Carbohydrate Counting" by Sheryl Salis, and standard food tables. They are estimates: dhaba sizes, oil and sugar vary a lot. Khurak only does the maths with the ratio you enter; it does not replace advice from your doctor or diabetes educator. Check your sugar regularly, and if the suggested number ever looks wrong to you, trust your own judgement and your team.
