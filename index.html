        About these numbers

        Carb values come from the ISPAD Indian foods carb counting booklet, the "Quick Guide to Carbohydrate Counting" by Sheryl Salis, and standard food tables. They are estimates: dhaba sizes, oil and sugar vary a lot. T1DTMD only does the maths with the ratio you enter; it does not replace advice from your doctor or diabetes educator. If a suggested number ever looks wrong, trust your own judgement and your team.
